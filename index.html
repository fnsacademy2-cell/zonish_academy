Google Firebase Cloud Sync (Access Records from Other Locations)
Local Storage Mode

Connect your Google Firebase Firestore database to access, view, and update student records, monthly fees, and expenses live from your home laptop, mobile phone, or branch computer.

Paste the code snippet from Firebase Project Settings -> Your apps
— OR FILL INDIVIDUAL FIELDS —
Quick 2-Minute Free Firebase Setup:
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click "Add Project" & name it (e.g. zonish-academy).
  3. In the left sidebar, click Build -> Cloud Firestore, then click "Create Database".
  4. Select "Start in test mode" (allows cloud read & write) and choose any location.
  5. Click the Gear icon ⚙️ (Project settings) -> Under "Your apps", click the Web icon </>, register app, and copy your firebaseConfig!
  6. Paste it into the box on the left and click "Connect & Start Live Sync".
✨ Once connected, any computer or phone where you open this page with the same config will sync in real-time!
